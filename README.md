## 🛡️ Why Install to Home Screen? (Addressing Student Hesitations)

Many students hesitate when a browser prompts them to *"Install App"* or *"Add to Home Screen"* because they worry it might be an unsafe download or an untrusted file. Here is why TrackMyCompany is **100% safe, verified, and vastly superior when installed**:

### 1. Zero APKs, Zero Suspicious Downloads
- TrackMyCompany is a standard **Progressive Web App (PWA)** governed strictly by browser security sandbox policies.
- It requires **no device permissions** whatsoever: no camera access, no contacts, no location, and no background mic.
- There is **no login, no password, and zero server-side tracking**. Everything is stored directly on your personal phone.

### 2. Why Home Screen is the #1 Most Reliable Way to Use It

## 📲 Step-by-Step Installation Guides

### Method 1: On Android (Google Chrome / Brave / Samsung Internet)

#### Option A: In-App 1-Tap Button (Fastest)
1. Open [https://trackmycompany.online](https://trackmycompany.online) in Chrome.
2. Tap the **Phone/Install icon** in the top navigation bar (or wait for the friendly *Add icon to home screen* modal).
3. Tap **"Know more"** to view privacy guarantees, then tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Chrome will confirm: tap **Add / Install**.

#### Option B: Via Chrome Three Dots Menu (⋮)
1. Open [https://trackmycompany.online](https://trackmycompany.online) in Chrome.
2. Tap the **Three Dots (⋮)** in the top-right corner of your browser.
3. Tap **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app"**.
4. Confirm by tapping **Add**.
5. The TrackMyCompany icon is now placed on your home screen and app drawer!

---

### Method 2: On iPhone & iPad (Apple Safari)
1. Open [https://trackmycompany.online](https://trackmycompany.online) in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share icon** (the square with an arrow pointing upwards) at the bottom toolbar.
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.
5. The app icon will now appear on your iOS home screen in standalone full-screen mode!

---

### Method 3: On Laptop & PC (Chrome / Edge / Brave)
1. Open [https://trackmycompany.online](https://trackmycompany.online) on your computer (make sure it is `https://` with the secure lock icon).
2. **Option A (Fastest — Address Bar):** In the right side of Chrome's address bar (URL bar), click the **Install icon** (monitor with a downward arrow) ➡️ click **Install**.
3. **Option B (Chrome Menu):** Click the **Three Dots (⋮)** ➡️ **"Cast, save, and share"** ➡️ **"Install TrackMyCompany"**.
4. **Option C (Universal Shortcut):** Click the **Three Dots (⋮)** ➡️ **"Cast, save, and share"** ➡️ **"Create shortcut..."** ➡️ check **"Open as window"** ➡️ click **Create**.
5. TrackMyCompany opens in an independent desktop window with its own icon. Pin it to your **Windows Taskbar** or **macOS Dock** for one-click access throughout placement season.

## 🎯 First-Time User Guide: Placement Season Workflow

TrackMyCompany is engineered around the actual daily realities of college placement drives. Here is how to make the most of it:

### 1. Adding a Company
- Tap the vibrant **+ (Add Company)** button at the bottom center.
- Fill in the company details:
  - **Company Name** (e.g., *Cisco Systems*, *Texas Instruments*, *Deltek*)
  - **CTC Package** (Flexible text: e.g., `18 LPA`, `8.5 + 1L Retention Bonus`, `30k/month stipend`)
  - **Drive Type / Tier**: Open Dream (≥12 LPA), Dream (<12 LPA), Mass Recruitment, or Internship.
  - **Status**: Choose between **Applied** or **Skipped**.

### 2. Grouping & Reordering Applied Companies
When you apply to companies, some will shortlist you for the Online Assessment (OA) test, while others may not shortlist you due to CGPA cutoffs or branch criteria:
- **Clean Separation**: Your applied list automatically places **active / shortlisted companies at the top**, followed by a discreet divider line separating companies where you were not shortlisted.
- **Minimal Red Dot Indicator**: Non-shortlisted company cards display a subtle red dot indicator so you can quickly see which companies are closed without visual clutter.
- **Group Drag-and-Drop**: Easily drag and drop cards within your active list to rank them in order of personal priority (P1, P2, P3).

### 3. Smart Upcoming Drives
- The **Upcoming Drive** banner displays the nearest scheduled test date with countdowns (`Today!`, `Tomorrow`, `In 3 days`).
- **Strictly Filtered for Relevancy**: The upcoming drive list **only includes companies you applied to and are eligible to write**. Companies you skipped or were not shortlisted for will never clutter your drive schedule.
- **Today's Drive Notification**: If any company drive is scheduled for today, a clean popup reminds you immediately upon opening the app so you never miss a test window.

### 4. Tracking Skipped Companies
- Whenever you toggle a company to **Skipped**, the app lets you select reason tags (*Low CTC*, *Strict Bond / Service Agreement*, *Location Not Preferred*, *Ineligible*, or custom notes).
- Tap the **My Stats** tab at any time to see visual breakdown charts of your application funnel and why you opted out of certain drives.

### 5. Excel & CSV Import/Export
- Found in the **Data** tab:
  - **Export to Excel (.xlsx) / CSV**: Download a backup spreadsheet anytime.
  - **Import Spreadsheet**: Upload your existing placement Excel sheet with smart automatic column matching.
  - **JSON Backup**: One-click download of your entire profile.

## 🔒 100% Privacy & Data Safety Guarantee

- **No Remote Database**: TrackMyCompany has no servers collecting your personal data.
- **Local IndexedDB**: All company names, notes, and statuses stay strictly inside your phone's browser storage.
- **No Account Required**: You don't have to sign in with Google or share any email address.
- **Persistent Storage API**: Explicitly requests the browser to never clear placement data during automatic cache cleanups.

## 🛠️ For Developers & Contributors

> [!NOTE]
> This section is strictly for developers who wish to inspect the codebase, run the project locally, or contribute improvements. Regular users should simply open [trackmycompany.online](https://trackmycompany.online).

### Prerequisites
- Node.js 18+ & npm

### Local Development Setup
```bash
# 1. Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/Shash-J/TrackMyCompany.git

# 2. Change directory
cd TrackMyCompany

# 3. Install dependencies
npm install

# 4. Start the local Vite development server
npm run dev
```

Open [http://localhost:5173](http://localhost:5173) in your browser.

### Building for Production
```bash
npm run build
npm run preview
```

### Automated GitHub Pages Deployment
Every commit pushed to the `main` branch automatically triggers the GitHub Actions workflow in [`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`](.github/workflows/deploy.yml), building the site and deploying it directly to [trackmycompany.online](https://trackmycompany.online) and the GitHub Pages mirror.
